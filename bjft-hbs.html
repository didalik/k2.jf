Basic Job Fair Test

This test has 2 parts. First, 2 Job Agents, each one offering echo job, connect to jF before your echo job request is sent from this browser. One of them is being matched and, if your handshake with it is successful, runs the echo job for you. The second one is still hanging, so you send another echo job request to jF and, having been matched with it, get another echo job done for you - assuming, of course, that your handshake with it was successful.

The second part of the test starts when you send a third job request. The request hangs there for a little while until the 2 Job Agents connect to jF again. One of them is being matched and, if your handshake with it is successful, runs the echo job for you. The second one is still hanging, so you send your final job request to jF and, having been matched with it, get another echo job done for you - assuming, of course, that your handshake with it was successful.

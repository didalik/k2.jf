Welcome to Kloud Of Trust - the cloud-native, role-based organization of coders and testers. At present, it is my hobby project comprising two more projects:

  • Cloudflare Job Fair; and
  • Stellar Help Exchange.

Cloudflare Job Fair matches Job Agents (digital content producers) with Job Users (digital content consumers) for a given job and facilitates the data flow between them. Stellar Help Exchange provides an equitable marketplace, where users pay agents for jobs done - or get full refund otherwise.

I believe these are the first two steps needed to make the Internet a better place for you and me. So, if you are a coder or a tester - join me! As a coder, you'll be rewarded by the users of your code. As a tester, you'll get paid for your resources.
